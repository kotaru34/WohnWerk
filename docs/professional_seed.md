# Generalized professional discovery seed

This document defines the initial professional neighbourhood used by WohnWerk's coarse job-discovery gate. It intentionally contains only generalized professional information needed by the product; it does not contain employer history, dates, addresses, or other biographical detail.

The seed is a **lower bound**, not a closed CV taxonomy. The real vacancy corpus must be allowed to surface adjacent concepts for later human review.

## Core role families

Strong or highly plausible starting families include:

- Maschinenbauingenieur / Mechanical Engineer
- Konstruktionsingenieur / Konstrukteur / Mechanical Design Engineer
- Entwicklungskonstruktion / Entwicklungsingenieur
- Technische Projektleitung / Project Engineering
- Produktentwicklung
- Berechnungsingenieur / structural-strength analysis
- Fahrzeugtechnik / Fahrzeugentwicklung
- Schienenfahrzeugtechnik / rolling-stock engineering
- Sonderfahrzeugbau
- Vorrichtungsbau / tooling / fixtures / jigs
- Anlagen- und Sondermaschinenbau
- Mechatronik and adjacent mechanical technical roles

Generic roles such as `Engineer`, `Techniker`, `Technical Specialist`, `Application Engineer`, `Systems Engineer`, `Project Manager`, or `Team Lead` require supporting engineering-domain or method/tool evidence before entering the durable local corpus.

## Engineering domains

Important domain evidence includes:

- Maschinenbau / mechanical engineering
- mechanical components and assemblies
- product and component development
- vehicle / automotive engineering
- special vehicles
- rail vehicles / rolling stock
- fixtures, jigs and tooling
- plant engineering / special machinery
- metal engineering
- welding and welded assemblies
- assembly and commissioning
- manufacturing / production engineering
- chassis, carbody and vehicle structures
- wheel development
- technical stage/mechanical systems where the role is genuinely mechanical

## Supporting but non-defining skills

The following may be useful vacancy concepts but must **not** make an unrelated vacancy pass the coarse gate by themselves:

- SAP S/4HANA
- SAP R/3
- MS Office
- generic agile/Scrum wording
- generic project meetings
- generic stakeholder language

They are supporting workplace skills rather than evidence that a vacancy belongs to the target mechanical-engineering neighbourhood.

## Negative-context damping

Terms such as software/backend/cloud, pure data/AI, CRM/Salesforce, sales/business development, HR/recruiting, or finance/accounting are not absolute bans. They are used only to prevent weak matches such as `Software Engineer - Automotive` or `Sales Engineer Automotive` from entering the corpus because of one incidental domain word.

If such a vacancy contains substantial real mechanical evidence (for example CAD + FEM + vehicle structures), it may still pass and should later be judged by the user-reviewed concept model.

## Discovery principle

The coarse gate should optimize for high recall **inside** this broad professional neighbourhood. It should keep plausible adjacent technical roles but reject obviously unrelated work.

The seed must evolve through corpus-driven concept discovery. Newly observed titles, tools, responsibilities and disciplines become reviewable concepts; they do not automatically become positive user preferences.
